BloodlinesCE

The 2004 Vampire: The Masquerade – Bloodlines, running on a new, open-source engine.

Same maps, same quests, same dialogue. Bloodlines CE reads them from your own copy of the game (GOG or Steam, with the Unofficial Patch) and runs them on Godot 4 and modern C++ in place of the original executables. It ships with no game files.

It is not playable from start to finish yet. The tutorial runs and combat is being matched to the original.

    What works today

    The world

    • Every map loads with world geometry, displacements, baked lightmaps, skyboxes, water, brush entities and static props, at hundreds of frames per second.
    • Doors, lifts, trigger zones, level transitions and ambient sound.
    • How visible you are in the dark follows the map's lighting. Twelve spots checked in the original all match.

    Your character

    • Character creation with all seven clans. Stats, feats, costs and experience come from the game's rule files.
    • Run, walk, sneak and jump speeds come from your clan's body model, as in the original: 188 units a second running forward, 205.5 running back.
    • Falls, jumps and view punch, ported from how the original handles them.

    Talking

    • Conversations with voices, lip sync, facial expressions, gestures and the original dialogue frame and fonts.
    • Clan-specific lines, including Malkavian replies, and the Persuasion, Intimidate, Seduction and Dominate options.

    Fighting

    • Melee for the player and NPCs, each weapon with its own animations. Dogs bite.
    • NPCs hear footsteps and gunfire. Every fight in the tutorial can be won.

    How the game behaves

    • Character rules: attributes, abilities, feats, clans and experience costs.
    • Disciplines.
    • Combat: melee, firearms, damage and NPC fighting.
    • Movement: speeds, jumps and falls, with the measurements.
    • World: entities, triggers, doors and level changes.
    • Script API: every function the quest scripts call, and how often.
    • Open questions: what isn't known yet and what has been tried.

    Command-line tools

    One dumper per format. Each takes a file, or a folder to check everything in it, archives included: inventory, vpkdump, texdump, bspdump, mdldump, dlgdump, lipdump and vmtdump.

    Each finished phase has a decision record. The documents themselves will be published with the source code.

    How it's made

    A port, not a remake. When the two disagree, the original is right until someone has measured it.

    • No game files go in the repository. You bring your own copy.
    • No decompiled code and no leaked Valve source. What's learned from the original game's binaries, data files and measurements is written up as plain-language specs first, and the code is written from those.
    • Behaviour is checked against the original by playing it: speeds, light levels and timings are measured in the 2004 game and compared.
    • Fixes from the Unofficial Patch are reviewed one by one. Each is either kept or reproduced as it was, and the decision is written down.
